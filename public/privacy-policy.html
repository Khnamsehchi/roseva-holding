Privacy Policy

This is a draft for the Roseva website. It is not a finished policy and must not be published as one. Complete it, then have it reviewed and approved, before the site launches.

Newsletter and contact forms

When submissions are enabled, the newsletter form stores the email address you enter, the time you subscribed, and the status of that subscription. The contact form stores the name, email, mobile number, company, and message you enter, together with a request identifier. Those entries are kept so Roseva can send the related email and honor an unsubscribe request.

A thank-you message means the submission was saved. It does not mean an email has already been delivered. You can unsubscribe from Roseva news and updates with the link in a newsletter email. Until this draft is replaced, it does not state a legal basis, retention period, or company address.

What this draft leaves out

A company address, registration, and legal name for privacy notices have not been provided for this draft, so none are stated here. Retention periods, legal bases, and privacy rights are not stated here either, because they still have to be written.
